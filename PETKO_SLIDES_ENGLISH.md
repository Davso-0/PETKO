# PETKO — English Presentation Script

## Slide 1: One Integrated Platform for Veterinary and Agropecuarian Care

**Slide Title**  
PETKO: Better Care, Smarter Operations

**Subtitle / Hook**  
A digital partner that unites customer experience, veterinary management, and livestock operations.

**Slide Bullet Points**
- **PETKO — Vet & Agropecuario:** designed for pet owners, veterinary businesses, pharmacies, farms, and livestock operations.
- One platform for product discovery, customer self-service, sales, inventory, branches, and role-based management.
- Support for dogs, cats, rabbits, birds, rodents, horses, cattle, pigs, and other farm animals.
- Specialized, simple, and organized: less administrative friction and more control in every location.
- An accessible experience with bilingual content and light or dark viewing modes.

**Presenter Script / Simple Explanation**  
PETKO is more than an online catalog. It is an integrated veterinary and agropecuarian platform that helps pet owners find products quickly while giving clinics and their teams a clear way to manage operations. It brings together pet care, pharmacy products, point of sale, stock, users, and branches in one connected experience. The result is a solution that adapts from an individual customer journey to a multi-branch veterinary operation.

## Slide 4: Secure Access for Customers and Teams

**Slide Title**  
One-Click Convenience. Protected Accounts.

**Subtitle / Hook**  
Flexible sign-in, strong account rules, and a smooth onboarding journey.

**Slide Bullet Points**
- Traditional registration with a complete name, valid email address, strong password, and privacy acceptance.
- Password requirements include at least eight characters, one uppercase letter, and one number.
- One-click Google sign-in connects or creates the customer account after identity verification.
- Password recovery uses a secure, time-limited email link.
- Team accounts are activated through an individual email invitation with terms acceptance and a 24-hour expiry window.

**Presenter Script / Simple Explanation**  
PETKO offers the right access experience for every type of user. Customers can create a traditional account or use Google for an instant, verified sign-in. Registration includes clear password and privacy requirements, while recovery links help users regain access safely. For administrative and operational staff, account activation is controlled through a personalized email invitation. This protects the organization by ensuring that each team role receives the correct access and sets a password before activation.

## Slide 5: Role-Based Dashboards Put the Right Controls in Every Hand

**Slide Title**  
Centralized Control. Personalized Workflows.

**Subtitle / Hook**  
Administrators oversee the business; branches run daily operations; customers stay in control of their reservations.

**Slide Bullet Points**
- **Administrators:** manage branches, roles, team accounts, product records, stock across locations, and overall sales reports.
- **Managers and cashiers:** control the assigned branch, inventory, registers, shifts, payments, and sales activity.
- **Customers:** view reserved products, branch pickup information, account details, and password security options.
- Inventory tools support category and stock filters, low-stock alerts, stock adjustments, and branch-specific availability.
- Access is role-based, while session protections and inactivity controls help keep operational areas secure.

**Presenter Script / Simple Explanation**  
PETKO gives each user the tools that match their responsibility. Administrators gain a company-wide view of branches, users, products, inventory value, and sales performance. Managers and cashiers work within their branch to adjust stock, operate registers, open shifts, process sales, and follow daily results. Customers receive a simpler dashboard to manage reservations and account settings. This role-based structure keeps the platform powerful for the business while remaining clear and easy to use for every person involved.
